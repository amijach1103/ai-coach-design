The 90-Day Cohort Experience

What it looks like to build something alongside people doing the same thing.

1

Joining

Week 0: before the first session

AI opportunity

Say where you're starting from

An entrance survey: what you're building, what you need, what you're worried about.

Observed: most complete it, a minority never do, and the ones who don't are the ones who go quiet in week 3.

AI opportunity

Write your first plan

Objectives for 90 days, written as outcomes you could be wrong about.

Observed: plans arrive late and vague. The bottleneck is not writing, it is deciding what to be judged on.

Human-essential

Meet everyone

Learn the rhythm and hear what everyone else is risking.

Observed: attendance is highest here and never again. Whatever this session does has to keep working without it.

2

The weekly cycle

Weeks 1–12: the same shape every week

Built

Write the weekly report

What moved, what didn't, why. Against the plan, not against the calendar.

Observed: roughly half submit in a given week. The automated nudge exists because the manual chase did not scale.

Human-essential

Read someone else's week and respond

Peer comment before the group session.

Observed: most read, far fewer comment. Reading is not the gap; going on record is.

AI opportunity

Prepare for the session

Arrive knowing what everyone else is stuck on.

Observed: preparation is the step that silently disappears first when the week is bad.

Human-essential

The group session

Present, get challenged, commit to a next step out loud.

Observed: the commitment made out loud is the one that gets done.

3

After

Week 13 onward: the part the design usually forgets

Human-essential

Keep going without the structure

The cadence ends. The work does not.

Observed: the group asked to continue past the end date, which is the strongest signal the programme produced and the one nothing in the design had planned for.

AI opportunity

Find out whether it worked

Measured against what each person said they were trying to do in week 0.

Observed: without the week-0 plan this question has no answer, which is why the plan is a touchpoint and not paperwork.
